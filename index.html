For Educational Purposes Only
Vedic Clock is an educational tool designed to introduce users to the Vedic time-keeping system rooted in ancient Indian astronomy and astrology. The information provided — including Muhūrtas, Pañchāṅga elements, Kāla periods, and Nakshatra data — is intended solely for general interest and learning.

No Professional Advice
Nothing in this application constitutes medical, legal, financial, astrological, or spiritual advice. Please consult a qualified Jyotiṣa practitioner or relevant professional before making important life decisions based on astrological timing.

Accuracy of Calculations
Sunrise, sunset, and planetary calculations are approximate. They use simplified astronomical formulas and may differ slightly from precise ephemeris data. Results vary by location; the app uses device GPS or a default location when GPS is unavailable.

Cultural Respect
The Vedic calendar system is a living tradition honoured by millions worldwide. This application presents a simplified modern interpretation. We encourage users to explore primary Vedic texts and experienced teachers for deeper understanding.
