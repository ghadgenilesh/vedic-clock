# Vedic Clock

**Live app → [vedicclock.stacklets.app](https://vedicclock.stacklets.app)**

A beautiful, real-time Vedic clock that brings the ancient Indian time-keeping system to your fingertips — rooted in the rhythms of the sun, moon, and stars.

---

## What is Vedic Clock?

Vedic Clock is a modern take on the traditional *Pañchāṅga* — the five-limbed Vedic almanac that has guided daily life in India for thousands of years. Instead of simply showing hours and minutes, it shows you *where you are in the cosmic day*: which muhurta you are in, what the moon is doing, and when the most auspicious moments of the day occur.

Whether you are planning an important meeting, beginning a creative project, or simply curious about Vedic wisdom, Vedic Clock gives you that layer of awareness at a glance.

---

## What's on the screen

### Clock tab
Your home view. A live clock sits at the centre showing the current time, the active *Kāla* (energy period), and which of the 30 daily *Muhūrtas* you are in right now. Scroll down for a full solar-arc ribbon and a colour-coded muhurta timeline for the whole day.

### Pañchāṅga tab
The five classical elements of the Vedic day, updated in real time:

| Element | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Vara** | The day of the week and its planetary ruler |
| **Tithi** | The lunar day — where the moon stands in its cycle |
| **Nakshatra** | The lunar mansion — the star the moon occupies |
| **Yoga** | The combined Sun–Moon energy pattern of the day |
| **Karana** | The half-lunar-day quality for fine-grained timing |

Below the five elements you'll find the day's **Kāla periods** — six energy zones from pre-dawn Brāhma Muhūrta through to evening Sāyaṃ Kāla.

### Muhūrtas tab
All 30 muhurtas of the day listed in order, each labelled **Auspicious**, **Neutral**, or **Avoid**, with a note on the kinds of activities each is best suited for. The current muhurta is highlighted so you always know where you stand.

### Sun Arc tab
A visual arc of the sun's journey across the sky for your location today, with exact times for:
- **Sūryodaya** — Sunrise
- **Madhyāhna** — Solar noon
- **Sūryāsta** — Sunset
- **Brāhma Muhūrta** — The 96-minute pre-dawn window considered the most auspicious time for meditation and spiritual practice

### About tab
Short, plain-language guides explaining what a Muhūrta is, how to read the Pañchāṅga, what the Kāla periods mean, and a primer on the 27 Nakshatra lunar mansions.

## Features

- **Real-time updates** — the clock, muhurta, and panchanga refresh every second
- **Location-aware** — uses your device's GPS automatically, or search any city in the world
- **Dark & Light mode** — a deep indigo night sky or a warm parchment day theme
- **12h / 24h time** — switch to whichever format you prefer
- **Muhurta change alerts** — optional browser notifications when a new muhurta begins
- **Works on any device** — phone, tablet, or desktop browser, no installation required

---

## A note on purpose

Vedic Clock is an educational tool. The calculations — sunrise, sunset, lunar position — use standard astronomical formulas adapted for your location and are a close approximation rather than precise ephemeris data. The app is designed to introduce you to the Vedic time-keeping tradition and is not a substitute for guidance from a qualified *Jyotiṣa* (Vedic astrology) practitioner.
