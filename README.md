# 🕉 Vedic Clock

> *Time as the ancients measured it — precise, sacred, and alive.*

Vedic Clock is a modern app that brings the ancient Indian timekeeping system to your phone and browser. It shows you not just *what time it is*, but **where you stand in the cosmic day** — the Ghati, the Nakshatra overhead, today's Tithi, and whether the moment is auspicious for what you're about to do.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🕰 Vedic Clock
The traditional Indian day is divided into **60 Ghatis** — each roughly 24 minutes long — counted from sunrise. The animated clock dial shows the current Ghati and Pala in real time, like a clock face reborn from the Vedic age.

### 📅 Panchang
The Panchang ("five limbs") is the Vedic almanac for every day. It tells you:
- **Vara** — the ruling weekday deity
- **Tithi** — the lunar day and whether it's waxing or waning
- **Nakshatra** — the lunar mansion the Moon occupies
- **Yoga** — the combined energy of Sun and Moon
- **Karana** — the half-tithi, for fine-grained timing

Each element is colour-coded: green for auspicious, red for inauspicious, so you can see at a glance what kind of day it is.

### ⏰ Muhurta & Kalam
Know *when* to act — and *when not to*:
- **Rahu Kalam** — the period ruled by Rahu, avoided for new beginnings
- **Gulika Kalam** — the period of Saturn's shadow
- **Yamaganda** — the period of Yama, inauspicious for travel
- **Abhijit Muhurta** — the powerful midday window, auspicious for almost anything

### 🪐 Graha (Planetary Positions)
See where all nine Vedic planets (Navagraha) are positioned in the sky right now — in their **sidereal (Vedic) positions**, corrected with the Lahiri Ayanamsa. Includes the Rashi Chakra showing which sign each planet occupies.

### 🎉 Festivals
A living calendar of upcoming Hindu festivals with countdowns — from Makar Sankranti to Diwali — so you're never caught off guard.

### 🌍 Works Everywhere
- Detects your location automatically for accurate local sunrise/sunset
- Full **dark mode** support with a traditional saffron-gold palette
- Works on iOS, Android, and in your browser
- Available in **7 languages**: English, हिन्दी, संस्कृत, తెలుగు, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ, मराठी

## 📖 Vedic Time — A Primer

If you're new to this system, here's what these words mean:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ghati** (घटी) | 1/60th of a day, counted from sunrise. One Ghati ≈ 24 minutes. |
| **Pala** (पल) | 1/60th of a Ghati ≈ 24 seconds. |
| **Vipala** (विपल) | 1/60th of a Pala ≈ 0.4 seconds. |
| **Tithi** | One of 30 lunar days in a month, based on the Moon–Sun angle. |
| **Paksha** | The fortnight — Shukla (waxing/bright) or Krishna (waning/dark). |
| **Nakshatra** | One of 27 lunar mansions the Moon passes through each month. |
| **Yoga** | A quality of the day derived by adding Sun and Moon longitudes. |
| **Karana** | Half of a Tithi — there are 11 types, each with a different nature. |
| **Vara** | The weekday, each ruled by a planet (Ravi=Sun, Soma=Moon, etc.). |
| **Rahu Kalam** | A ~90-minute daily window considered inauspicious for new work. |
| **Muhurta** | An auspicious window of time, used for scheduling important events. |
| **Panchang** | Literally "five limbs" — the Vedic almanac combining Vara, Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana. |
| **Ayanamsa** | The correction applied to convert Western (tropical) positions to Vedic (sidereal) ones. This app uses the Lahiri/Chitrapaksha standard. |
| **Graha** | "That which grips" — the nine Vedic celestial bodies: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu (North Node), Ketu (South Node). |
| **Rashi** | One of the 12 zodiac signs in Vedic astrology. |

## 🙏 Philosophy

> *"Kālo jagat-bhakṣakaḥ"* — Time is the devourer of worlds. — Bhagavad Gita 11.32

Vedic timekeeping is not just about precision — it is about **living in rhythm with nature**. Sunrise and sunset anchor the day. The Moon governs the emotional tide of each fortnight. The Nakshatras mark the sky's slow rotation through the year. This app is an attempt to make that ancient awareness accessible to everyone, everywhere.
